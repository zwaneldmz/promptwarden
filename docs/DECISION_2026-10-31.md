## Pre-registered kill bar (write `docs/DECISION_2026-10-31.md` on day 1, never edit)

A commitment counts only as: signed pilot agreement + org legal entity + seat count ≥ 50 +
start date before 2026-12-15 + named exec sponsor and IT owner + deployment method +
documented works-council status, and **either** a paid pilot fee (€1,500, credited to year 1)
**or** a written convert-or-decline date. An MSP counts only with a named ≥50-seat end-client
and a scheduled deployment date. Enthusiasm scores zero.

## Amendment 2026-07-25 (pre-freeze, per council review)

An adversarial council reviewed this repo and the 90-day plan on 2026-07-25, ahead of the
2026-07-27 freeze. Two changes below tighten the pre-registered bar; both are additive to the
verbatim block above, not edits to it.

### (a) Revenue floor on qualifying commitments

Per-row eligibility is unchanged: every qualifying commitment still needs every element of the
bar above, including **either** the paid pilot fee (€1,500, credited to year 1) **or** a
written convert-or-decline date.

Added, portfolio-level: of all commitments that count toward the kill criterion (the rows
tallied as counting in the Tally section below), **at least half must carry actual paid
revenue** — the €1,500 paid pilot fee, or paid-assessment revenue from a delivered Shadow-AI
Exposure Check engagement (€1,500–2,500, per `docs/NEXT_PLAN.md`'s W3 SKU). A written
convert-or-decline date with no revenue attached may not account for more than half of the
counted rows. This closes a gap the council flagged: a funnel that clears the bar on
enthusiasm-adjacent paperwork (decline dates, no money changing hands) would satisfy the
letter of the original bar without the revenue signal the bar exists to test for.

### (b) Pre-registered G5 metric — "sensitive hit" definition

`docs/NEXT_PLAN.md` gates G5 (09-20) on "median exposure ≥5 sensitive hits per 10
users/week." Pre-registering the definition now, before the freeze, so it can't be loosened or
tightened in week 8 when the number is inconvenient either way:

A **sensitive hit** counts only a match from these detectors: `credit_card`, `iban`,
`at_svnr`, `api_key`, `bulk_pii`. `email` and `phone` matches are excluded from this metric —
they fire on routine business correspondence and would inflate the exposure number without
indicating shadow-AI risk.

The metric is measured on whatever host coverage the deployment actually has at the time of
measurement (i.e. whichever AI/chat sites are in that install's active/matched host list —
not a hypothetical full-coverage set). Every Exposure Check report and the G5 scoring itself
must state that coverage explicitly (which hosts were monitored) alongside the hit count, so a
low number from narrow coverage is never read the same as a low number from broad coverage.
See `docs/HOST_COVERAGE.md` for what coverage is actually shipped as of a given date — as of
this amendment, every exposure number the repo can produce is a 7-default-host number.
